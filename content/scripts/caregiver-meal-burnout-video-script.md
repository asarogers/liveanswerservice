# Video Script: Caregiver Meal Burnout

**Format:** Short-form video (Instagram Reels / TikTok / YouTube Shorts)
**Duration:** ~30–40 seconds when spoken at a natural pace
**Speaker:** Ace Rodgers, Live Answer

---

## Script

You're already cooking for your own family — and then driving to your mom's to start all over again.

That daily grind is what burns caregivers out. Not one big crisis. Just Tuesday. And Wednesday. And every day after.

Here's what changes it: one afternoon a week. A batch of protein, roasted vegetables, a grain, and one soup. That's 15 to 20 meals — labeled, portioned, ready to reheat. No daily cooking. No guilt.

## Notes for Filming

- Warm, direct eye contact — speak *to* the caregiver, not at them
- Pause slightly after "Just Tuesday. And Wednesday." — let it land
- Show hands portioning containers during the solution section if possible (B-roll)
- CTA card on screen at the end with URL
