## The after-hours missed-call problem

The 5pm-to-bedtime window is when working customers finally have time to call — and it's exactly when the office is empty. A caller who hits voicemail rarely leaves a message; they call the next listing instead.

For some verticals the after-hours call isn't just a sale — it's an emergency. A burst pipe, a furnace that quit on a cold night, a roof leaking during a storm, a dental abscess, a tenant locked out. These callers will reach *someone* tonight. The only question is whether that someone is you.

## Who actually answers after 5pm

Each option has a real failure mode worth understanding:

- **Voicemail.** Cheap and silent. The overwhelming majority of callers hang up rather than leave a message, and an emergency caller absolutely will not wait for a callback.
- **A personal cell on call.** Works until it doesn't — the owner is asleep, driving, or on another job, and burnout is real. It also means no consistent intake or record.
- **Legacy human services.** Ruby, AnswerConnect, Posh, and Reliable Receptionist do answer after hours, but "24/7" usually means 24/7 *routing*. At 3am the call may hit a smaller overflow center, a generic script, or a queue. And the pricing — $245–$400+/mo base plus per-minute fees, often $1,600/mo effective and up to $3,200–$4,000/mo for full human coverage — makes round-the-clock staffing expensive.
- **24/7 AI.** Answers every call live, in parallel, no queue, at any hour, for a flat rate.

## What to look for in an after-hours service

Not all "24/7" is equal. When you evaluate an after-hours answering service, check that it:

- **Answers live with no queue** at every hour, including holidays — not just routes a call.
- **Charges a flat rate.** Per-minute pricing punishes you exactly when after-hours volume spikes. Live Answer is $500/mo flat, unlimited calls, no overages, rate locked at sign-up.
- **Is bilingual at no upcharge.** In California, after-hours emergency callers are often Spanish-speaking. EN/ES should be standard, never a premium.
- **Knows when to wake you.** Urgent calls transfer to a real person; everything else is captured for the morning. You shouldn't have to choose between missing emergencies and getting woken for spam.
