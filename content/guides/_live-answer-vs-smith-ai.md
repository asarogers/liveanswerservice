If you want predictable flat-rate pricing, unlimited calls, and bilingual EN/ES with direct booking into your CRM, **Live Answer is the better fit**. If you specifically want US-based human agents handling some of your calls and you're comfortable with usage-based pricing that scales with volume, **Smith.ai is the better fit**. Both are credible choices — this guide draws the lines fairly so you can decide.

## What each service actually is

Smith.ai is an established AI-plus-human hybrid receptionist service with a strong reputation and a long track record. Its agents are US-based, its integration library is broad, and many small businesses trust it precisely because a real person can step in on a call. That human layer is a genuine strength, and we won't pretend otherwise.

Live Answer is a done-for-you, fully managed AI receptionist. We build and operate the agent for you. It answers every call 24/7 — nights, weekends, holidays — books appointments, captures and qualifies leads into your CRM, filters spam, transfers urgent calls to a person, and sends an SMS summary after each call. It's built for California small businesses in the trades, legal, dental, and similar verticals.

## Coverage

Both services cover after-hours. The structural difference is how calls scale. AI doesn't queue: with Live Answer, every call is answered in parallel in under two rings, whether it's one call or twenty at once during an HVAC heatwave rush. Hybrid services route some calls to human agents, which introduces the possibility of a queue at peak volume. Smith.ai's human coverage is a feature when you want a person; it's a constraint when call volume spikes.

## Pricing model

This is the clearest dividing line. Live Answer is **$500/mo flat, unlimited calls, no per-minute fees, no overages**, with your rate locked at sign-up. Setup is $199 (waived on annual prepay; annual runs $5,100/yr). There's a 7-day free trial with no card.

Smith.ai's pricing is usage-based — plans are tiered by call volume, and the effective cost tends to climb as you take more calls. That model is fine if your volume is low and steady. But for a busy SMB, usage-based billing makes your monthly cost a moving target. With a flat rate, a heavy month costs the same as a light one.

## Bilingual (EN/ES)

Live Answer includes native English/Spanish at no upcharge. The agent detects the caller's language in the first few seconds and runs the full intake — including CRM sync and SMS summary — in Spanish. For a California business with Spanish-speaking customers, this is often decisive. Confirm Smith.ai's current bilingual terms with them directly, as offerings and surcharges vary by plan.

## Integrations

Live Answer writes directly into the platforms California SMBs actually use: Jobber, ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, FieldEdge, Clio, MyCase, Dentrix, Open Dental, AppFolio, Mindbody, and Square Appointments. The booking lands in your dispatch board or case system before you've read the SMS summary. Smith.ai also has a strong, broad integration library — this is an area where both are competitive, and the right answer depends on your specific stack.

## Where each one wins

**Smith.ai wins** when you require a named US-based human on the line, when your call patterns are nuanced enough that real-time human judgment matters, or when your volume is low enough that usage-based pricing stays cheap.

**Live Answer wins** when you want every call answered with zero queue 24/7, a flat predictable bill regardless of volume, bilingual EN/ES included, native CRM booking, and a managed setup you don't have to configure yourself. For a San Jose trades or dental practice fielding 200–500 calls a month, the flat-rate AI math is usually dramatically better.
