## What each service actually is

Ruby Receptionists is a premium, well-known human virtual receptionist service. Its reputation is built on friendly live agents and polished brand experience — callers reach a real, warm person, and for many businesses that human touch is exactly the point. That warmth is a genuine strength.

Live Answer is a done-for-you, fully managed AI receptionist. We build and run the agent for you. It answers every call 24/7 — nights, weekends, holidays — books appointments, captures and qualifies leads into your CRM, filters spam, transfers urgent calls to a person, and sends an SMS summary after every call. It's designed for California small businesses in trades, legal, dental, and similar verticals.

## Coverage

Ruby's live agents cover business needs well during staffed hours and offer after-hours plans. With any human service, though, calls can queue at peak volume, and around-the-clock live coverage typically costs more. Live Answer's AI doesn't queue: every call is answered in parallel in under two rings, at 3 AM Sunday or during a Monday-morning rush, at the same flat cost.

## Pricing model

This is the sharpest difference. Ruby is priced by receptionist-minutes — among the most premium options in the category — and your monthly cost moves with how many minutes your callers consume. Some customers report frustration with how minutes and overages are billed; we'd frame that simply as billing-model complaints some customers report, and we'd encourage you to read Ruby's current terms closely. We're not asserting any specific allegation or figure.

Live Answer is **$500/mo flat, unlimited calls, no per-minute fees, no overages**, with the rate locked at sign-up. Setup is $199 (waived on annual prepay; annual is $5,100/yr), and there's a 7-day free trial with no card. A flat rate means a busy month and a quiet month cost the same — no minute-counting, no overage surprises.

## Bilingual (EN/ES)

Live Answer includes native English/Spanish at no upcharge — the agent detects the caller's language within seconds and handles the full intake, CRM sync, and SMS summary in Spanish. For a California business with Spanish-speaking customers, this is frequently the deciding factor. Confirm bilingual terms with any human service directly, as staffing and surcharges vary.

## Integrations

Live Answer writes bookings directly into the SMB platforms California businesses use: Jobber, ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, FieldEdge, Clio, MyCase, Dentrix, Open Dental, AppFolio, Mindbody, and Square Appointments. The appointment is in your dispatch board or case system before you read the summary. Human services like Ruby generally relay messages and can integrate with some tools, but native, write-through booking into vertical SMB systems is where managed AI has the edge.

## Where each one wins

**Ruby wins** when human warmth is core to your brand promise, when your clients expect a polished live person every time, and when you're willing to pay premium per-minute rates for that experience — high-end professional services and relationship-driven practices often value this.

**Live Answer wins** when you want every call answered with no queue 24/7, a flat predictable bill regardless of volume, bilingual EN/ES included, native CRM booking, and a fully managed setup. For a San Jose HVAC company or dental office fielding hundreds of calls a month, the flat-rate math and no-queue coverage are usually decisive.
